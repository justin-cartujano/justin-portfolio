Not just a certificate. A real pathway.

Get certified in the full Sculpt method. Here’s exactly what you walk away with:

  • The full method, not the basics. You’ll learn the anatomy, the muscle activation, the cueing, and how to actually structure and lead a class.
  • Real practicum, real reps. You’re not just listening. You teach during the training and get real-time coaching. By the time you finish, you’re not wondering if you can do it. You know you can.
  • Taught personally by Monica del Valle. She trains with precision and real intention, and she stays involved after you certify. You keep her support as you grow.

All graduating students get the opportunity to audition for an instructor role on the Break Free team. Seats are limited to keep the coaching and practicum real for every trainee.
